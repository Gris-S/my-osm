Tap the name of a town or neighbourhood on the map: its outline is highlighted,
with population, area, density, region, and directions.

Places with a Wikipedia article (towns, museums, monuments, stations) show its
summary and photo, in your language when it exists. Offline areas can take
them along, photos optional, refreshed monthly.

Tapping an empty spot on the map now places the pin at once; the address
follows.
