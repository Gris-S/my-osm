Two fixes, both found by driving the app on a real phone.

Opening the app recentres the map on your position again. It had quietly
stopped: the check guarding it asked the Android WebView a question that gets
the same answer whether location is allowed or not.

Denied location is now explained on screen, and points at your phone's
settings, instead of a button that spins for ten seconds and then says nothing.

The release walkthrough grew from nine checks to twenty-two.
