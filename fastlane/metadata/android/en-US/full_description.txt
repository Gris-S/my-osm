A map that keeps your places to itself.

MY OSM shows shops and their opening hours, live public transport departures,
step-by-step navigation and offline maps — built on OpenStreetMap, with no
account, no tracking, and nothing sent to Google.

FIND PLACES
Shops, cafés, pharmacies, transport and more, sorted into 16 categories you can
switch on and off. Opening hours tell you whether a place is open right now, not
just what its hours are. When OpenStreetMap does not know a place, a built-in
browser searches the web and brings the location back to the map — without ever
querying Google.

GET THERE
Walking, driving and public transport, plus a running mode with its own pace
chart. Driving navigation shows live traffic on your route, speed limits, lane
guidance and speed-camera warnings, and it can control whatever music you are
playing. Walks and runs are kept on the device, with their track and elevation
profile, for as long as you choose — or not at all.

RIDE
Live departures worldwide through Transitous, and in the Paris region through
Île-de-France Mobilités. Each departure says whether it is measured or merely
timetabled. Journeys are followed step by step: where to board, which direction,
where to get off, which station exit to take.

GO OFFLINE
Download a country, a region or a department: map tiles, place details, the
search index and street addresses are stored inside the app, where the system
cannot clear them. Browsing, search and place details then work with no
connection at all.

One honest limit: route calculation still needs a connection. There is no
routing engine on the device, and the app says so plainly instead of failing
silently.

AND THE REST
Weather, air quality, pollen and official French weather warnings. Street-level
photos from Mapillary. Saved places in colour-coded folders. Home and work as
one-tap destinations. Light and dark themes, the dark one following the phone's
ambient light sensor. French and English.

PRIVACY
No Firebase, no Analytics, no Play Services, no Google Maps. Android's cloud
backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for this app, and the
WebView's own telemetry and Safe Browsing are turned off. The built-in browser
blocks every Google host outright. There is no account to create and no server
to leave to: saved places, home and work addresses, trip history and downloaded
maps stay on the device.

Some extras can use free API keys (Île-de-France Mobilités, TomTom, Mapillary,
Météo-France). They are entered in the app, stored on the device, and are never
compiled into a published build. Without them the app still works — public
transport falls back to Transitous everywhere, and each extra simply says what
it is missing.

This is alpha software: it works, it is used daily, and it still changes.
